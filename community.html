COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

A space built on respect and curiosity.

🤗

Be welcoming

All levels are welcome here. No gatekeeping, no elitism — we were all beginners once.

✅

Be accurate

Share facts, not assumptions. If you're not sure, say so — honesty helps everyone learn better.

💡

Be helpful

Answer the question asked. Point to good resources. Help people take the next step forward.

🚫

No spam or self-promotion

This is a learning community, not a marketplace. Keep content relevant and useful.
